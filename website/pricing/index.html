Prices follow your App Store region (Lifetime often shown around $29.99 USD before tax). Subscriptions cancel anytime in system settings. X Browser does not use in-app advertising to monetize Free or Premium. Learn more on the ad-free browser page.

What you get on Free

Free is meant to be usable every day: up to three Spaces with full cookie and login isolation, built-in tracker and ad blocking, and no in-app ads in the product chrome. That is enough for personal + work + one extra account without upgrading.

When Premium helps

Upgrade when you need more than three Spaces, or when a Space should stay behind Face ID, Touch ID, PIN, or pattern. Choose Yearly or Monthly if you want a subscription, or Lifetime for a one-time purchase. Premium still does not add advertising—billing is App Store only.

FAQ

Is Free forever free?

Yes. The Free plan stays available with up to 3 Spaces and tracker blocking. Premium is optional.

Where do I cancel a Premium subscription?

Manage or cancel Yearly/Monthly in your Apple ID / App Store subscription settings. Lifetime is not a subscription—there is nothing to cancel after purchase.

What does Lifetime include?

Lifetime is a one-time purchase for the same Premium benefits: unlimited Spaces and Space Lock. It does not auto-renew.

Does Premium change privacy claims?

No. Spaces still do not change your IP or claim anonymity. Premium mainly adds capacity (unlimited Spaces) and Space Lock.
