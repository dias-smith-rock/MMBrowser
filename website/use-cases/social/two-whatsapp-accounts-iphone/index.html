How to use two WhatsApp accounts on iPhone

Run two WhatsApp Web logins on one iPhone with XBrowser Spaces—no clone apps. Isolated cookies keep chats from mixing.

Download XBrowser How Spaces work

The pain

Official WhatsApp is one account per app install. Clone apps feel risky, and Safari usually means log out / log in.

How XBrowser solves it

Create two Spaces (Personal / Work). Open WhatsApp Web in each Space once. Switch with the Space chip—or Split View both at once.

Step-by-step

  1. Install XBrowser and create Space A (Personal) and Space B (Work).
  2. In Space A, open web.whatsapp.com (or m.whatsapp.com) and scan/link your first account.
  3. Switch to Space B and link the second WhatsApp account the same way.
  4. Optional: open Split View with each pane bound to a different Space.
  5. Optional: Space Lock the Work Space if those chats are sensitive.

Does this replace the WhatsApp app?

You use WhatsApp Web inside XBrowser. Features follow WhatsApp’s web client, which can differ from the native app.

Will the two accounts share cookies?

No—each Space has its own cookie jar. That is the point of Spaces.

Does this hide my IP from WhatsApp?

No. Spaces isolate on-device sessions. They do not change network IP.

Spaces isolate cookies and local libraries on your device. They do not change your IP or bypass platform rules. Use XBrowser to avoid mix-ups—not to violate site terms.
