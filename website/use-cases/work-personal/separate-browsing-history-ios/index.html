Keep browsing history separate on iOS

XBrowser stores history per Space so work research doesn’t pollute personal suggestions—and vice versa.

Download XBrowser How Spaces work

The pain

Shared history leaks context into autocomplete and “recently visited.”

How XBrowser solves it

History is isolated per Space along with cookies and bookmarks.

Step-by-step

  1. Browse work topics only in Work Space.
  2. Keep personal browsing in Personal Space.
  3. Check History inside each Space to confirm separation.

Private Browsing vs Spaces?

Private tabs are temporary. Spaces keep long-lived separated libraries.

Spaces isolate cookies and local libraries on your device. They do not change your IP or bypass platform rules. Use XBrowser to avoid mix-ups—not to violate site terms.
