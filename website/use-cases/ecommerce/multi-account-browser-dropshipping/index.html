A multi-account management browser for dropshipping

XBrowser Spaces keep supplier, store, and ad logins isolated on iOS—built for dropshippers who juggle many panels.

Download XBrowser How Spaces work

The pain

Suppliers, storefronts, and ads dashboards fight over one cookie jar on mobile Safari.

How XBrowser solves it

Map each role to a Space. Switch without logging out. Add tracker blocking for cleaner panels.

Step-by-step

  1. Space map: Storefront / Supplier / Ads / Support.
  2. Sign into each panel only in its Space.
  3. Turn on Block Ads & Trackers where it helps readability.
  4. Upgrade to Premium if you need more than 3 Spaces.
