E-commerce & Sellers

Search intent: prevent cross site tracking for multiple seller accounts

Reduce cross-site tracking across multiple seller accounts

Combine Space isolation with built-in ad & tracker blocking so seller sessions share less local state on iPhone.

Download XBrowser How Spaces work

The pain

Shared cookies and trackers can stitch activity across storefronts when everything lives in one profile.

How XBrowser solves it

Separate Spaces cut shared cookies. Block Ads & Trackers reduces many tracker requests. Still not a VPN.

Step-by-step

  1. Put each seller identity in its own Space.
  2. Enable Block Ads & Trackers globally (or per page when needed).
  3. Avoid opening unrelated shops in the same Space.
  4. Clear a Space’s data if a session feels contaminated.
