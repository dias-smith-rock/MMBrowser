Privacy & Session Isolation

Sandbox sessions, cookie isolation, and multi-login containers.

  • Session isolation browser for iPhone

    XBrowser isolates sessions with Spaces—separate cookies and libraries—plus tracker blocking and Space Lock.

  • A sandbox browser for iOS multi-login

    Sandbox-style Spaces on iOS keep multi-login sessions from sharing cookies—built for WhatsApp, shops, and email.

  • Separate cookies across sessions on iPhone (via Spaces)

    XBrowser isolates cookies per Space—not per tab inside one Space. Use Spaces when you need different cookie jars on iPhone.

  • Multiple independent web sessions in an iOS browser

    Run multiple independent web sessions on iOS with XBrowser Spaces—parallel logins, separate storage, optional locks.
