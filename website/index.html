Why XBrowser

Built for how you actually juggle accounts

One browser, multiple identities. Each account keeps its own session space—without pretending to hide your IP.

Social accounts that don’t mix

Keep Instagram, X, or YouTube logins in separate identities. Switch profiles without logging out—or leaking cookies across accounts.

Run multiple shops, cleanly

Manage different store or seller accounts in one browser. Each shop keeps its own cookies, history, and bookmarks.

Work stays at work

Use a dedicated work identity for email and docs. One tap on the account chip switches you back—history and home stay separate too. Optional Split View shows two accounts at once so you can see isolation for yourself.
