How it works

Isolation you can feel in one tap

Spaces are the product. Everything else—locks, media tools—builds on that isolation.

  1. 01

    Create a Space

    Add Messaging, Shop, Work, or a custom identity. Each gets its own cookies and site data.

  2. 02

    Browse in that Space

    History, bookmarks, and home stay with the Space you are in—so work does not bleed into personal.

  3. 03

    Switch with the chip

    Tap the Space chip to jump identities. Sessions stay put until you delete the Space.

Space separation does not change your network IP or make you anonymous online. XBrowser focuses on practical session isolation and local data control.

What is a Space?

A Space is an isolated browsing identity. It has its own cookies, site data, history, bookmarks, and tabs—so accounts do not mix.

How many Spaces are free?

Free includes up to 3 Spaces. Unlimited Spaces and Space Lock need Premium. See Pricing.

Does Space isolation hide my IP?

No. Spaces isolate cookies and logins on your device. They do not change your network IP or make you anonymous online.

Can I lock one Space only?

Yes. Space Lock (Premium) can protect a single Space with PIN, pattern, or biometrics while other Spaces stay unlocked.

Does XBrowser show its own ads?

No. XBrowser does not monetize with in-app ads. Built-in blocking reduces many web ads and trackers (best-effort). Advanced features are funded by optional Premium. See Ad & tracker blocking.

Can web video play in the background?

Yes—for supported streams, the built-in player offers Background Audio, floating playback, and Picture in Picture. Results depend on the site. See Built-in player.
