Why MMBrowser

Built around how you actually browse

Three practical controls—identity, clutter, and what stays on the device—without pretending to make you invisible on the internet.

Dual Accounts

Keep separate logins and cookies for work and personal. Spoof or deny location per account—so sites see the identity you choose. Location spoofing only affects browser geolocation APIs; it does not change your network IP.

Fewer Ads & Shorts

Block ads and trackers as you browse. Turn on Shorts Focus to hide YouTube Shorts shelves and stay on the videos you want. Filters are best-effort and may need updates when sites change.

Lock & Clear

Protect MMBrowser with Face ID, PIN, or pattern. Auto-clear browsing data when you leave—so nothing lingers on a shared device.
