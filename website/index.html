Why XBrowser

Built for how you actually juggle accounts

One browser, multiple identities. Each account keeps its own session space—without pretending to hide your IP.

Multiple WhatsApp & IG logins

Run multiple web sessions for WhatsApp Web, Instagram, Telegram, and Facebook—each in its own account so cookies and logins don’t mix. Uses each site’s web version inside the browser; features may differ from the official apps.

Run multiple shops, cleanly

Manage different store or seller accounts in one browser. Each shop keeps its own cookies, history, and bookmarks.

Work stays at work

Use a dedicated work identity for email and docs. One tap on the account chip switches you back—history and home stay separate too.

How it works

Isolation you can feel in one tap

Each account is a separate browsing space on your device. You stay signed in where you need to—without mixing sessions.

  1. 01

    Create an account

    Add Messaging, Shop, Work, or a custom identity. Each gets its own cookies and site data.

  2. 02

    Browse in that space

    History, bookmarks, and home stay with the account you’re in—so work doesn’t bleed into personal.

  3. 03

    Switch with the chip

    Tap the account chip to jump identities. Sessions stay put until you delete the account.

Account separation does not change your network IP or make you anonymous online. XBrowser focuses on practical session isolation and local data control.
