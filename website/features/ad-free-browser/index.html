No ads in the product itself

Many “free” browsers insert banners, interstitials, or app-open ads. XBrowser does not. There are no AdMob-style placements in the chrome, no interstitial paywalls disguised as ads, and no advertising SDK monetizing your sessions.

Built-in ad & tracker blocking

Separately, XBrowser filters known ad and tracker network requests and can hide common on-page banners. The address-bar shield reflects protection. Blocking is best-effort—sites change—but the goal is cleaner pages while you use Spaces.

How we fund the app

Free includes core Spaces and blocking. Premium unlocks unlimited Spaces and Space Lock via App Store subscription—not by showing you more ads.

Is XBrowser really free of in-app ads?

Yes. XBrowser does not use in-app advertising as a monetization path. You will not see app-open or interstitial ads from us while using the browser.

Does that mean every website is ad-free?

No. Built-in blocking reduces many web ads and trackers, but some sites and video players may still show ads. Blocking is best-effort and may change when sites update.

How do you make money?

Optional Premium (unlimited Spaces and Space Lock) via the App Store. Free remains usable without watching ads to “earn” features.
