Core capability · Ad-free browser

No ads in the app. Fewer ads on the web.

X Browser is an ad-free browser in two ways: the product itself shows no in-app ads on Free or Premium, and built-in Block Ads & Trackers reduces many ads and trackers on sites you visit (best-effort). We don’t sell your browsing history. Premium only unlocks more Spaces and Space Lock—never by showing you ads.

Download the ad-free browser See Free vs Premium

No ads in the product itself

Many “free” browsers insert banners, interstitials, or app-open ads. X Browser does not. There are no AdMob-style placements in the chrome, no interstitial paywalls disguised as ads, and no advertising SDK monetizing your sessions. Crash and usage analytics stay off until you opt in.

Built-in ad & tracker blocking

Separately, X Browser filters known ad and tracker network requests and can hide common on-page banners. The address-bar shield reflects protection. Blocking is best-effort—sites change—but the goal is cleaner pages while you use Spaces.

How we fund the app

Free already has no in-app ads, plus core Spaces and blocking. Premium only adds unlimited Spaces and Space Lock—still no in-app ads.

Honest limits

Privacy Policy →
Is X Browser really free of in-app ads?

Yes—on Free and on Premium. X Browser does not use in-app advertising as a monetization path. You will not see app-open or interstitial ads from us while using the browser. We also don’t sell your browsing history or profile you for advertisers.

Do Free users see ads until they upgrade?

No. The Free plan does not show in-app ads. Premium is optional and only unlocks unlimited Spaces and Space Lock.

Do you track me for ads?

No advertising SDK tracks you for monetization. Optional crash and analytics sharing is off by default and never required to browse. See What X Browser collects.

Does that mean every website is ad-free?

No. Built-in blocking reduces many web ads and trackers, but some sites and video players may still show ads. Blocking is best-effort and may change when sites update.

How do you make money?

Optional Premium (unlimited Spaces and Space Lock) via the App Store. Free stays fully usable with no in-app ads—you never watch ads to “earn” features.
