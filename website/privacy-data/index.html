What XBrowser collects (and what stays on your iPhone)

XBrowser is built so most browsing data stays on your device. This page is the short version of the Privacy Policy.

On-device by default

History, cookies, and Space data stay on the iPhone unless you erase them or use an OS backup you control. Passwords in the vault are not synced to XBrowser servers.

Operational services

If you leave “Share crash & analytics data” on (the default), Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us fix crashes and understand which features are used. Remote Config can update filter and product flags. You can turn crash and analytics sharing off in Settings. Core browsing does not require it.

Purchases

The App Store handles Premium purchases. XBrowser does not see your full payment card details.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your browsing history.
  • We don’t show in-app ads.
  • We don’t change your IP or claim anonymity.
