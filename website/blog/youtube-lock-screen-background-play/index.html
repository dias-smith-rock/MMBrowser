How to play YouTube (web) in the background on lock screen

Keep web YouTube playing after lock on iPhone, and pair it with Sleep Timer for auto-stop.

Overview

  1. Open web YouTube in XBrowser and start playback.
  2. Lock the screen once the media session is active and confirm audio continues.
  3. For bedtime auto-stop, turn on Sleep Timer.
