Bedtime podcast & white-noise power guide: timed stop for web audio

Save battery at bedtime with web podcasts and white noise—background play plus Sleep Timer fade-out so audio does not run all night.

The power trick: stop on time

All-night white noise keeps media and network sessions alive. A Sleep Timer fades volume and stops playback—less wasted drain.

Recommended flow

  • Open your usual white-noise or podcast page.
  • Enable background play, then set a 20–45 minute timer.
  • Lock and sleep; playback stops when time is up.
