Guide · Multi-account

How cross-border sellers can safely run 5 accounts on one phone

Use a multi-account sandbox browser to isolate cookies and manage multiple shop and social logins on one iPhone.

The pain: one browser, constant logouts

Classic browsers share one cookie jar—switching accounts means logging out, and it is easy to post in the wrong shop. Isolated Spaces give each login its own cookies and history.

Practical tips

  • One shop per Space with clear names (Shop-A / Shop-B).
  • Turn on Space Lock for sensitive shops.
  • Validate the flow with 3 free Spaces, then upgrade for unlimited.
